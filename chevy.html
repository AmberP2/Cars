About Chevrolet

Chevrolet, most known as Chevy, is an American automaker company founded in 1911 by Louis Chevrolet and William C. Durant. The company belongs to the General Motors family (GM).

Chevrolet is known for making a variety of vehicles, including cars, trucks, SUVs, and sports cars. The company sells vehicles in many countries around the world.

Popular Chevrolet Vehicles

  • Chevrolet Silverado - Full size pickup truck
  • Chevrolet Corvette - Sports car
  • Chevrolet Camaro - Muscle car
  • Chevrolet Tahoe - Large SUV
  • Chevrolet Equinox - Compact SUV

Interesting Facts

  • Chevrolet was founded in 1911.
  • The company logo is called the "Bowtie."
  • The Corvette has been produced since 1953.
  • Chevrolet is one of the best known American car brands.
