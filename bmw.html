History & Heritage

BMW (Bayerische Motoren Werke AG) Bavarian Motor Works was officially founded on March 7, 1916, in Munich, Germany. BMW originated from aircraft engine and vehicle manufacturers Rapp-Motorenwerke and Bayerische Flugzeugwerke. BMW produced aircraft engines during World War 1 and transitioned to producing cars in 1923. Today Munich’s Oberwiesenfeld district remains home to BMW’s main plant and headquarters.
