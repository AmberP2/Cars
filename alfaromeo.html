Alfa Romeo

About Alfa Romeo

Alfa Romeo is an Italian luxury car brand founded by Cavalier Ugo Stella in 1910. The company operates under the Stellantis automotive group. Alfa Romeo is known for its luxury vehicles with their own unique visual style. The company sells vehicles in many countries around the world.

  • ALFA originally stood for Anonima Lombarda Fabbrica Automobili (Lombard Car Manufacturing Company). The "Romeo" part of the name was added in 1920 after Nicola Romeo took over the company during World War I.

About Top Models

Alfa Romeo Guilia

The 2026 Alfa Romeo Giulia is a luxury compact sports sedan featuring sharp handling, a driver-focused layout, and a turbocharged 2.0-liter 4-cylinder engine that generates 280 horsepower and 306 lb-ft of torque. Extra specs include a 2 liter turbocharged inline 4 engine, 280 horsepower @ 5,200 rpm and 306 lb-ft of torque, a top speed of 149 mph, and a 0-60 of 5.5 seconds.

Alfa Romeo Tonale

he 2026 Alfa Romeo Tonale is a stylish compact luxury SUV featuring a 2.0-liter turbocharged four-cylinder engine producing 268 horsepower and 295 lb-ft of torque, paired with a 9-speed automatic transmission and standard Q4 all-wheel drive.

Alfa Romeo 8C Competizione

The Alfa Romeo 8C Competizione is a limited-edition Italian sports car produced from 2007 to 2009 (with the open-top Spider variant running through 2010) Featuring a 4.7-liter naturally aspirated Ferrari/Maserati (F136 YC) V8 engine, 450 hp (331 kW) at 7,000 rpm and 480 N·m (354 lbf·ft) of torque, a 6-speed automated manual (robotized sequential) transmission, front mid-engine, rear-wheel-drive (transaxle layout), a top speed of approximately 292 km/h (181 mph), and a 0–60 of roughly 4.2 seconds.

Alfa Romeo Stelvio Quadrifoglio

The 2024 Alfa Romeo Stelvio Quadrifoglio is a high-performance luxury compact SUV known for its Ferrari-derived powertrain and sports-car-like handling, featuring a 2.9-liter twin-turbocharged V6 (Ferrari-derived) engine, 505 hp @ 6,500 rpm, 443 lb-ft @ 2,500–5,500 rpm, 8-speed ZF automatic transmission, all-wheel drive (AWD) with a rear-biased setup, a 0–60 mph of 3.6 seconds, a top speed of 176 mph, and a curb weight of 4,313 lbs.
